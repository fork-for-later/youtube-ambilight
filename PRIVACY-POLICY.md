# Privacy policy
- This extension only runs on tabs that start with the url https://www.youtube.com. The extension will only activate the ambient light effect on YouTube's /watch page
- The extension does not send crash reports, error logs, settings, video URLs, or identifiers to Sentry or other third parties. Errors may appear in your browser's local developer console. To analyze video content, the extension may load storyboard images from YouTube using URLs provided by YouTube; this is part of the extension's video functionality and is not sent to a separate analytics or error-reporting service.
